Remember that iconic scene from Pulp Fiction, where Vincent is so careless as to ask for _"a please would be nice"_ before getting to work. The scene is legendary. It [encapsulates everything that's important about consultancy](/stories/the-bonnie-situation/), advise and mentorship - The morale: You can't help anyone, unless they truly _want_ to be helped.
{: .kicker}

|Vincent: |  “Please” would be nice.|
|Wolf:    |  Come again?|
|Vincent: |  I said “a please would be nice”.|
